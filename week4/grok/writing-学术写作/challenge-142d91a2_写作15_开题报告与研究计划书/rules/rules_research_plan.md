# DOMAIN RULES — 开题报告与研究计划书 (kind=research_plan)

## Focus
- 立得住: question specific & falsifiable; background truly points to the gap (known-unknown-to-verify), not a literature dump.
- 对得上: goals, route, verification, expected outcomes correspond to the question.
- 排得开: stage plan fits given period/budget/data/personnel; milestones match workload.
- 说得清: risks have trigger signals + fallbacks; claims/citations traceable.

## Rules
1. Extract constraints/known conclusions; form gap statement; converge to 1-3 answerable questions with assessable criteria.
2. Decompose route into stages with input/output/dependency; schedule within constraints.
3. Align goal-route-verification-outcome; check tuples complete.
4. List risks with trigger + alternative; keep claims/citations source-bound.

## Self-check
- [ ] gap real; question falsifiable
- [ ] goal/route/verification/outcome aligned
- [ ] schedule feasible; milestones match
- [ ] risks with triggers+fallbacks; editable output
- [ ] no facts/citations/numbers outside source; gaps flagged
- [ ] editable deliverable + provenance/claim_map + change log; result.json honest
