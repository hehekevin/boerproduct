# Evening blue light delays sleep onset in adults (2018 trial)

Source-id: S1
Year: 2018
DOI: 10.0000/example.s1
Status: SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE DOCUMENT (fictional; not a real publication).

[P1] In this randomized crossover trial of 40 adults, evening blue-light exposure (480 nm, 2 hours) delayed sleep onset latency by 28 minutes compared with dim-light control.

[P2] Melatonin onset was suppressed by 45 minutes under blue light. The effect was reversible after a 3-day washout.
