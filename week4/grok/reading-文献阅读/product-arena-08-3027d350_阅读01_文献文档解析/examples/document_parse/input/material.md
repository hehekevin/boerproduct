[P1] Thermal Annealing of Perovskite Films for Stable Solar Cells

[P2] A. Example_author and B. Sample_researcher (Demo University)

[P3] Abstract: We study the effect of thermal annealing on perovskite film stability. Annealing at 100 C for 10 min improved crystallinity without decomposition.

[P4] Methods: Films were spin-coated and annealed at 100 C for 10 min in nitrogen. XRD patterns were recorded before and after annealing.

[P5] Results: The annealed films retained 95% of initial efficiency after 500 h, versus 70% for unannealed controls.

[P6] Conclusion: Moderate thermal annealing improves perovskite film stability under the tested conditions.
