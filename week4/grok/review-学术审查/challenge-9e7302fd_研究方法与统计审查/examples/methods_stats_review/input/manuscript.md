# Dose-Response Study of Compound X (SYNTHETIC DEMO MANUSCRIPT)

We measured the response of eight samples at increasing doses of Compound X.

Eight samples were measured. No sample size calculation was performed.

The mean response was 12.0 (SD 1.5). A statistical test showed the treatment effect was significant (p = 0.03).

Response increased with dose (slope 0.29, R2 0.29). The 95% confidence interval upper limit was 13.04.
