# DOMAIN RULES — 基金申请书写作 (kind=grant_proposal)

## Focus
- 立得住: necessity argues why worth doing & why now (others' progress, gap, why us), not policy slogans.
- 说得准: innovation states difference vs specific prior work; no unverifiable '首次/国际领先/填补空白'.
- 对得上: goals, content, technical route, assessment indicators correspond one-to-one.
- 查得到: key claims & references traceable.

## Rules
1. Derive necessity from field status/bottleneck/applicant's prior observation.
2. Converge to answerable, bounded scientific questions; decompose into assessable goals.
3. Align goal-content-route-indicator tuples; check completeness (no orphan goal/route/indicator).
4. State innovation against cited prior work; keep references traceable; flag missing applicant evidence.

## Self-check
- [ ] necessity defensible
- [ ] innovation verifiable vs prior work
- [ ] goal/content/route/indicator aligned
- [ ] references traceable; editable output
- [ ] no facts/citations/numbers outside source; gaps flagged
- [ ] editable deliverable + provenance/claim_map + change log; result.json honest
