EGF binding to the extracellular domain of EGFR induces receptor dimerization and autophosphorylation. Activated EGFR recruits GRB2 via phosphotyrosine residues. GRB2 activates SOS1, which promotes RAS activation through GDP-GTP exchange on RAS. RAS activates RAF, initiating the RAF-MEK-ERK kinase cascade. Phosphorylated ERK translocates into the nucleus, where it phosphorylates the transcription factor ELK1. ELK1 activates transcription of the FOS gene. SPRY2 inhibits SOS1, forming a negative feedback loop. ERK also inhibits RAF, providing a second negative feedback loop.
