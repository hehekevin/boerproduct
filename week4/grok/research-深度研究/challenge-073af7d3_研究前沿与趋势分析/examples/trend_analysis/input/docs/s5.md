Year: 2022
DOI: 10.0000/example.s5
Status: SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE DOCUMENT (fictional; not a real publication).

[P1] A dose-response study showed that evening screen luminance above 100 lux shifted circadian phase by up to 90 minutes, with a nonlinear threshold near 30 lux.

[P2] Short-wavelength content mattered more than total luminance below the threshold.
