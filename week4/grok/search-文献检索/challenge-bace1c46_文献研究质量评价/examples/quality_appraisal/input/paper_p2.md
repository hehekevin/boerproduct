# SYNTHETIC paper P2 — Observational diet cohort

## p2-para1 (Methods, page 1)
We followed 300 volunteers recruited from a single clinic and compared diet patterns with glucose outcomes over 2 years.

## p2-para2 (Results, page 4)
Higher fiber intake was associated with lower 2h glucose (beta=-0.3, 95% CI -0.5 to -0.1); adjustment details are not described in this excerpt.
