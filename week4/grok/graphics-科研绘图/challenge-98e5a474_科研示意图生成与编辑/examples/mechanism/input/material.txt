Section 2.1 — EGFR–MAPK cascade.
Epidermal growth factor receptor (EGFR), a receptor tyrosine kinase at the plasma
membrane, activates the small GTPase RAS upon ligand binding. Active RAS recruits
and activates the kinase RAF. RAF phosphorylates and activates MEK, which in turn
phosphorylates and activates ERK. Activated ERK translocates to the nucleus and
activates the transcription factor ELK1, driving proliferation gene expression.
The phosphatase DUSP6 dephosphorylates and thereby inhibits ERK, forming a negative
feedback loop. All relations are stated as established in this example text.
