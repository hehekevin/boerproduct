Title: Treatment X elevates marker expression over time in a controlled cohort.

Background / question (Sec 1): Whether Treatment X raises marker expression relative
to control, and how the effect evolves over 14 days, is unresolved.

Methods (Sec 2): Two arms (Control, Treatment), n=6 per arm per timepoint; expression
measured at Day3, Day7, Day14; values are mean +/- SD (data.csv).

Results (Sec 3, Table 1 / data.csv): Control stays near baseline (1.0, 1.1, 0.9).
Treatment rises from 1.8 (Day3) to 3.2 (Day7) to 4.5 (Day14). The Day14 treatment
mean is 4.5x the control Day14 mean (0.9).

Limitations (Sec 4): single cohort, n=6, no mechanistic assay; SD reported, not SEM.

Conclusion (Sec 5): Treatment X increases marker expression in a time-dependent
manner in this cohort; mechanism remains to be tested.
