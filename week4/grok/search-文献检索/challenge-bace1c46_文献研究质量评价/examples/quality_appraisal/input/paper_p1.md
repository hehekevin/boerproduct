# SYNTHETIC paper P1 — Exercise RCT in prediabetes

## p1-para1 (Methods, page 2)
We randomized 120 adults with prediabetes 1:1 to a 12-week exercise program or usual care, using a computer-generated sequence with allocation concealment.

## p1-para2 (Methods, page 3)
Sample size was calculated for 80% power to detect a 0.5 mmol/L difference in 2h glucose (alpha=0.05); 110 participants were required and 120 enrolled.

## p1-para3 (Results, page 5)
The primary analysis used intention-to-treat with mixed models; dropout was 8% and balanced across arms.
