# Blue-light filtering shows no sleep-onset effect in adolescents (2020)

Source-id: S3
Year: 2020
DOI: 10.0000/example.s3
Status: SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE DOCUMENT (fictional; not a real publication).

[P1] In 80 adolescents, blue-light filtering lenses produced no significant change in sleep onset latency (mean difference 2 minutes, 95% CI -6 to 10).

[P2] Adherence was low (52% of nights), and the authors caution that null results may reflect poor compliance rather than absence of effect.
