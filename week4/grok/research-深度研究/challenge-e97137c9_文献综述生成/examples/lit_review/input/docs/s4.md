# Meta-analysis of blue-light filtering and sleep onset (2021)

Source-id: S4
Year: 2021
DOI: 10.0000/example.s4
Status: SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE DOCUMENT (fictional; not a real publication).

[P1] This meta-analysis of 9 trials (n=520) found a pooled reduction in sleep onset latency of 12 minutes with blue-light filtering, with substantial heterogeneity (I2=68%).

[P2] Effects were larger in adults than adolescents, and in trials with objective adherence monitoring.
