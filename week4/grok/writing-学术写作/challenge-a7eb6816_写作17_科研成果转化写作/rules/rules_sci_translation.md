## Focus
- 读得懂: target reader understands without extra background (no unexpanded abbreviations/unexplained formulas/jargon).
- 留得住: key conclusions/data/limits/sample/metric口径/figure refs preserved; drop redundancy not evidence.
- 说得准: claim strength matches source (no correlation→causation, preliminary→definitive); one term one meaning.
- 接得住: genre conventions of the target form met.

## Rules
1. Identify audience knowledge/purpose/needs; set explanation depth/term density/analogy/examples/length.
2. Prioritize must-keep (conclusions/limits/method points/data) vs compressible vs deletable; never drop evidence.
3. Keep numbers/units/comparisons/limits; match claim strength to source; unify terminology.
4. Adapt to target genre structure; record what was compressed/removed and why.
