[P1] Introduction: Does thermal annealing improve the operational stability of perovskite solar cells?

[P2] Data: We used 30 spin-coated perovskite films (1 cm2 each) from a single batch.

[P3] Methods: Films were annealed at 100 C for 10 min in nitrogen; controls were unannealed. Efficiency was tracked under 1 sun illumination at 25 C.

[P4] Results: Annealed films retained 95% of initial efficiency after 500 h, versus 70% for controls.

[P5] Conclusion: Under the tested conditions, moderate thermal annealing improves operational stability.

[P6] Limitations: Only one batch and one illumination condition were tested; outdoor degradation was not studied.
