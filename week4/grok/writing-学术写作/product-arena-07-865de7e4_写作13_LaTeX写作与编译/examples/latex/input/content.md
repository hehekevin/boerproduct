Title: Treatment X elevates marker expression
Author: Example Author

Introduction: Marker expression is a key readout. Treatment X increased expression 4.5-fold at Day14 (Ref1: Example A, 2024, J Example, doi 10.1234/ex.2024.001). Prior work reported similar dynamics (Ref2: Example B, 2023, J Example, doi 10.1234/ex.2023.002).

Methods: Two arms, n=6 per timepoint; expression measured at Day3, Day7, Day14; reported as mean +/- SD.

Results: Treatment rose to 4.5 by Day14 versus control 0.9.

Conclusion: The increase is time-dependent in this cohort; mechanism remains open.
